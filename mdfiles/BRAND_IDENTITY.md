# Brand Identity — KarmiqLabs

## Name Analysis: `karmiqlabs`

### Strengths
- Unique — very likely available as domain
- "Karma" has cultural resonance in India, internationally understood
- "Labs" signals innovation and experimentation
- One word, reasonably memorable

### Weaknesses
- The `q` in "karmiq" will be misspelled constantly (karmiclabs, karmiqlabs, karmyclabs)
- Doesn't communicate your service category at first glance
- Sounds more like a dev tool/product company than a services firm
- Hard to say out loud confidently in sales calls

### Verdict
**Usable if you've already committed to it (social handles, Upwork profile, etc.)** If not yet locked in, consider these alternatives.

## Brand Positioning

### One-Line Pitch
"We migrate your cloud and modernize your stack — cheaper, faster, with zero downtime."

### Tagline Options
1. **"Move smart. Scale free."** — migration + freedom from cloud bills
2. **"From legacy to leading."** — modernization focus
3. **"Cut cloud costs. Kill tech debt."** — direct, pain-focused
4. **"Your escape from AWS bills and dead stacks."** — bold, edgy

**Recommended:** "Cut cloud costs. Kill tech debt." — addresses both pain points directly.

### Brand Voice
- Direct, not corporate
- Confident without being arrogant
- India-built, world-class quality
- Show the numbers (savings, timelines, ROI) — always lead with proof
- Avoid: buzzwords (synergy, leverage, holistic), vague claims

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

```
Primary:    #0F172A  (Slate 900 — deep navy, trust, depth)
Accent:     #6366F1  (Indigo 500 — tech, modern, distinct)
Highlight:  #10B981  (Emerald 500 — growth, savings, success)
Warning:    #F59E0B  (Amber 500 — cost alerts in ROI calculator)
Background: #F8FAFC  (Slate 50 — clean, light)
Text:       #1E293B  (Slate 800 — readable, not harsh black)
```

### Typography
```
Headings:  Inter (700/800 weight) — clean, technical, professional
Body:      Inter (400/500 weight) — same family, consistent
Mono:      JetBrains Mono — for code snippets, terminal-style cost displays
```

### Logo Concept
- Icon: Two overlapping arrows forming a shift symbol (→↑) or a stack of rectangles sliding right
- Text: Company name in Inter 700, all lowercase
- Colors: White on dark, dark on light
- Keep it simple — no complicated illustrative logos

### Design Aesthetic
- Dark hero section with light body
- Cards with subtle borders and hover states
- Terminal/code-style callouts for technical details
- Green numbers for savings amounts (psychological anchor)
- Before/after cost comparisons in the case study
- Minimal, no clutter — think Linear.app, Vercel's own site style
